    Where these numbers come from

      Listing numbers are for homes listed for sale (asking prices), not final sale prices, and are for the Gulfport-Biloxi metro area unless a county is named. "Typical" means the median: half of homes are above it and half below. Mortgage rates are the national weekly average for borrowers with strong credit; your rate may differ. Information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed.
