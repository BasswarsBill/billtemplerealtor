Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

This policy covers billtemplerealtor.com and the "Bill Temple Realtor" Facebook Page, run by Bill Temple, a Realtor with Epique Realty.

What I collect

Only what you choose to send me. When you fill out a form on this site (contact, home value, home wish list, Ask Bill, or a buyer or seller questionnaire), I receive the details you type in, such as your name, phone number, email address, the property or homes you're asking about, and your signature on forms that ask for one.

The site doesn't use advertising trackers, and it doesn't ask you to create an account.

How I use it

To answer you and help with your real estate questions or transaction. Form answers are emailed to me through an email delivery service (Resend). The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

I don't sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing. I may share details with others only when you ask me to as part of your transaction (for example, a lender or title company), or when the law requires it.

Facebook

This site uses a small Facebook app, "Bill Temple Realtor Post", only to publish posts (market updates, mortgage rates, neighborhood and buying or selling tips) to my own Facebook Page. The app does not collect, read or store any information about people who view, like or comment on those posts.

Deleting your information

To see, correct or delete what you've sent me, email bill@billtemplerealtor.com or call or text (601) 953-6504. I'll delete it within 30 days, except for anything I'm required to keep for a real estate transaction under Mississippi law or my brokerage's record-keeping rules.
