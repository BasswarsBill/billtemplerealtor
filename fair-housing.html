My fair housing commitment

Bill Temple and Epique Realty are committed to the letter and spirit of the U.S. policy for equal housing opportunity. I will help every buyer, seller, renter and investor the same way, and I do not discriminate against anyone because of:

  • Race or color
  • Religion
  • Sex, including sexual orientation and gender identity
  • Disability
  • Familial status (having children under 18, or being pregnant)
  • National origin

These protections come from the federal Fair Housing Act, which applies in every part of Mississippi. As a REALTOR®, I also follow the National Association of REALTORS® Code of Ethics, which adds protections for sexual orientation and gender identity in my own conduct.

I can't steer you toward or away from a neighborhood, and I can't answer questions about who lives in an area, its religious or ethnic makeup, or whether it's "a good area for families." The neighborhood guides on this site stick to facts like home prices, public data and things to do, so you can decide for yourself.

If you think your rights were violated

You can file a free complaint with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) within one year of what happened:

  • Online at hud.gov/fairhousing
  • By phone at 1-800-669-9777 (TTY 1-800-927-9275)

Concerns about a licensed real estate agent in Mississippi can also go to the Mississippi Real Estate Commission.

Accessibility

I want this website to work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, captions or zoom. I aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

If any part of the site is hard to use, or you'd like a form, guide or listing in another format, tell me and I'll get you the information another way right away. Email bill@billtemplerealtor.com or call or text (601) 953-6504.
